How to See Stripe Subscription Renewals

SaneSales reads your Stripe charges. Every paid charge becomes an order, so each subscription renewal shows up and counts toward revenue. What SaneSales doesn't do is mark a charge as a renewal or a first payment. Here is how to find renewals anyway, and what the app can't show you.

Find Renewal Payments

  • 1
    Connect Stripe in Settings with your secret key, from Developers, then API keys, in the Stripe Dashboard. The Stripe guide has the details.
  • 2
    Open Orders. If you have more than one store connected, pick Stripe in the provider filter. Then pick a range: Today, 7D, 30D, All or Custom.
  • 3
    Search for the customer's name or email. Their charges list by date. A monthly plan should show one charge a month.
  • 4
    Open an order to see the amount, the card brand and a Receipt link.

What SaneSales Shows

  • ✓
    Each paid Stripe charge as one order, with its amount, date, and the customer's name and email from the charge's billing details.
  • ✓
    An order name taken from the description Stripe stored on the charge. If a charge has no description, it shows Stripe Payment. The product breakdown groups orders by that name.
  • ✓
    Renewal charges in your revenue totals, charts and widgets, minus any refunds.

Telling a Renewal From a New Sale

Use the customer's history. If the same email paid you for the same plan before, a new charge from them is most likely a renewal. A first charge from an email you haven't seen is most likely a new subscriber. When you need to be sure, look the customer up in your Stripe Dashboard, which shows their subscription and invoices.

What It Can't Show

  • ×
    No label that says a charge is a renewal or a first payment. SaneSales doesn't read Stripe invoices or subscriptions.
  • ×
    No MRR, ARR, churn, count of active subscribers, or next renewal date.
  • ×
    No failed renewals. SaneSales lists only charges that were paid.

For those numbers, use the Subscriptions page in your Stripe Dashboard. SaneSales is for the money that came in: today, this month and over any range, next to your Lemon Squeezy and Gumroad sales.

Gumroad renewals show the same way, as one order per charge. Lemon Squeezy renewals don't show at all: Lemon Squeezy bills them as subscription invoices, not orders, and SaneSales reads orders. Only the first payment of a Lemon Squeezy subscription appears.

Questions

Does SaneSales count Stripe renewals in revenue?

Yes. Each paid renewal is a Stripe charge, and SaneSales counts every paid charge, minus any refund.

Can SaneSales show which payments are renewals?

No. It treats every Stripe charge the same way. Search for a customer in Orders to see their payments in date order.

Does SaneSales show MRR?

No. It shows revenue for days, months and custom ranges. It doesn't calculate MRR, ARR or churn.
